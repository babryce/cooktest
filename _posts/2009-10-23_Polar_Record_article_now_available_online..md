## Polar Record article now available online.

October 23, 2009

The [full text version of the article](http://journals.cambridge.org/download.php?file=%2FPOL%2FPOL45_04%2FS0032247409008419a.pdf&code=dd0868defa6df5dee6bbfbf916a408e1) detailing the recovery of Frederick A. Cook’s original telegram drafts announcing that he had attained the North Pole is now available online.  If the link does not work, simply Google Frederick Cook telegrams.

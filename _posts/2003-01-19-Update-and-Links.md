## Update and Links


Several interesting and detailed articles have appeared in this [scholarly journal](http://www.dioi.org/) concerning Dr. Cook’s claim to have climbed Mt. Mckinley; they are in volume 7, #2-3. [Additional articles](http://www.dioi.org/) on the counterarguments and methods of the Frederick A. Cook Society can also be found in this journal; they are in volume 9, #2-3.  
Many interesting links to polar subjects can be found on the website of [the Byrd Polar Research Center at Ohio State.](http://www-bprc.mps.ohio-state.edu/)
